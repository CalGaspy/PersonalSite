Custom PC Build

Hardware assembly • Custom PC building • Component compatibility planning

Project Overview

I needed a desktop for gaming and productivity. I selected compatible parts, including an Intel i5-12400 and RTX 4060, then assembled a liquid-cooled PC with room for future upgrades.

Key Features

  • RTX 4060
  • 16 GB of RAM
  • Liquid Cooled CPU

What I Learned

I learned to compare parts for compatibility before assembly, especially the CPU, motherboard, cooling system, and case. Installing liquid cooling and routing cables showed me how component placement affects airflow and future maintenance.
